## Post 3  ·  03_workflow.png
### From Excel to recovered rupees — in 5 steps.

This is the entire Fundle workflow. That's the whole pitch.

**Upload → Calculate → Reconcile → Flag → Recover.**

- Drop the marketplace file. The parser normalises Amazon, Myntra, AJIO,
  Nykaa, Tata Cliq and Flipkart into one schema.
- The rule engine computes what each order *should* have settled at —
  category, sub-category, price band, level, zone. No guesswork.
- Reconciliation runs at the SKU × settlement-line level.
- Discrepancies are flagged with a severity, a reason, and a rupee number.
- Recovery packs export straight to the marketplace claims format.

Median cycle time? **Minutes, not weeks.**

## Publishing tips

- **Aspect ratio**: images are 1024×1024 (LinkedIn's optimal for feed).
- **First-line hook**: LinkedIn truncates after ~140 chars — the first line
  of every post is written to survive the truncation.
- **Hashtags**: 10–14 per post is the current LinkedIn sweet spot.
- **CTA link**: put `fundlezone.com` in the *first comment*, not the body,
  to keep reach high. Native LinkedIn suppresses posts with external links
  in the body.
- **Cadence**: 1 post/week for 5 weeks. Re-share Post 4 & Post 5 as
  carousel posts after week 5.
