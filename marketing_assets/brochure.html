The Problem

Marketplace finance is a spreadsheet maze.

Every marketplace hands you a different XLSX, in a different format, at a different cutoff, with fees and commissions that are almost — but not quite — what the contract says. Every month, finance teams re-invent the same reconciliation from scratch. The rupees you're owed get lost in VLOOKUPs.

The Solution

One ledger. Six marketplaces. Every rupee reconciled.

Marketplace AutoPilot ingests every marketplace file natively, computes what each order should have settled at from your contract rate card, and flags the variance — before the month closes. Discrepancies come out as recovery-ready claim packs.
